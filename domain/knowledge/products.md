# Product descriptions

- Example Money Market Fund: very low risk, short-term deposits and treasury bills, suitable for holding cash.
- Example Government Bond Fund: low to medium risk, Israeli government bonds, moderate interest-rate sensitivity.
- Example Tel Aviv 35 Index Fund: higher risk, tracks the 35 largest companies on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange.
- Example Global Equity Fund: higher risk, diversified international equities, currency exposure.

Descriptions are general information, not a recommendation for any individual customer.
