# Withdrawal rules

- Minimum withdrawal: 500 NIS.
- Requests are processed within 3 business days of approval.
- Funds are transferred only to the bank account registered in the customer name.
- A request is "pending" until approved, then "completed". A request can be "rejected" if the balance is insufficient.
- Withdrawals submitted after 14:00 or on Friday/Saturday count as the next business day.
